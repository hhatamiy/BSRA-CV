# Equipment Checklist

Physical items the CV team needs this semester, roughly in the order
they become blocking. Check items off as they're acquired. Add a note
with who's responsible or the purchase status.

## Needed before November (camera calibration milestone)

| Item | Notes | Owner / Status |
|---|---|---|
| [ ] **Camera matching the robot's onboard camera** | Calibration parameters (intrinsics, distortion) are specific to one exact camera + lens. Confirm the exact model with the hardware/mechanical subteam before buying anything. If integration-team can get their own unit of the same model (rather than sharing the one on the robot), they can calibrate on their own schedule instead of waiting for robot assembly. | |
| [ ] **Printed checkerboard calibration pattern** | Standard OpenCV calibration target. Free to generate and print, doesn't need budget, just don't forget it. | |
| [ ] **Simple tripod or fixed mount** | For consistent, repeatable shots of the checkerboard at different angles during calibration. | |
